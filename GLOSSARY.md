# Warhammer Nameplates

Generates 3D-printable nameplates that identify painted Warhammer miniatures on display.

## Language

**Nameplate**:
A printed strip shaped to sit flush on the sloped rim of a model's base, bearing the model's name in raised capital letters. Its length is set by the name, not fixed.
_Avoid_: Label, tag, plaque (as a standalone term)

**Base**:
The round plastic disc a model is glued to, identified by its nominal diameter (e.g. a 40 mm base). Its sloped outer edge is the **Rim**.
_Avoid_: Stand, plinth (a plinth is a separate display block)

**Rim**:
The sloped outer side of a base, where a nameplate is glued.
_Avoid_: Edge, side

**Base Profile**:
The shape of a base given by its top diameter, bottom diameter and height; the rim's slope follows from these.
_Avoid_: Base size (that is only the nominal diameter)

**Base Preset**:
A named, approximate base profile for a standard nominal size (e.g. 32 mm), used when no measured profile is supplied.

**Model**:
A single painted Warhammer miniature that a nameplate identifies, e.g. Pedro Kantor.
_Avoid_: Mini, figure
